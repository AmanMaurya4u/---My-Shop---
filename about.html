About Arun Electronics

Arun Electronics is a trusted and well-known electronics shop located in Gopiganj, Bhadohi, Uttar Pradesh. We have been serving the community with quality electronic products and expert repairing services for years.

Our mission is simple — provide genuine products at affordable prices with excellent customer service. From televisions and refrigerators to coolers and electrical accessories, we have everything you need under one roof.

We also specialize in repairing services — whether it's a TV display issue, fridge cooling problem, or electrical wiring work, our skilled technicians handle it all with care and expertise.
